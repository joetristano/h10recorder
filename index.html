Zero-phase filters: 0.5 Hz high-pass, 40 Hz low-pass, 60 Hz notch. Analyze a recording or load this app’s CSV.
Red dots are detected R-peaks; orange dots are intervals more than 20% off their neighbours. They are flagged and left out of HRV, not diagnosed. They can be real early beats or detection errors, so check the trace. Grid: 0.04 s and 100 µV per small box. The strap records one lead, so no 12-lead view is faked. Motion (|a|) is shown under the ECG to help spot movement artifacts.
CSV columns: stream, t_s, v1, v2, v3. ECG: v1 = µV. ACC: v1,v2,v3 = x,y,z mG. HR: v1 = bpm, v2 = RR ms (one row per beat interval). HRV: summary rows at the end (v1 = value, v2 = metric name). Zone: seconds per heart-rate zone (v1 = seconds, v2 = zone). Share is the most reliable route on iPhone.
